Privacy Policy

Ad Hell (com.myadhellgame.adhell)
Developer: BogdanBobok · Effective date: 14 September 2026

This privacy policy explains what data the Ad Hell mobile application (the “Application”) collects, why it is collected, and who it is shared with. BogdanBobok (the “Service Provider”) operates the Application as an independent developer.

Data the Application collects

The Application does not ask you to register, does not create an account, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

The following data is collected automatically by the advertising software built into the Application and is shared with Google:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable identifier assigned to your device by Android. Used to select and display advertisements, to limit how often the same advertisement is shown, and to detect invalid traffic and fraud.
  • In-app interaction data — records of advertisements shown and tapped inside the Application, including when you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement in exchange for continuing your game. Used to display advertisements and measure their performance.

The Application is fully supported by advertising and does not offer any in-app purchase to remove it. Advertisements — banner, full-screen, and optional rewarded — are shown throughout the Application as part of how it works.

Game data stored on your device

The Application keeps a small amount of data locally so that it can remember how you left it. This data stays entirely on your device, is never uploaded or transmitted anywhere, and is not readable by the Service Provider. It consists of your best score, the selected theme (light or dark), and the selected interface language.

The “Our apps” screen also caches the names and icons of the developer’s other applications so that it works without an internet connection. All of this is removed when the Application is uninstalled.

Network use

Apart from advertising, the Application connects to the internet for one further purpose: the “Our apps” screen loads the names and icons of the developer’s other applications from their public Google Play pages. These requests contain no information about you or your use of the Application. The screen works without an internet connection, using data included in the Application itself and refreshed at most once a day.

Third-party services

The Application uses the following services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

Advertising choices

The Application requests advertisements from Google AdMob for all users, regardless of region. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Brazil, or another region where the law gives you a choice over ad personalisation, you can exercise it through your device’s own advertising settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android devices) and through Google’s own controls at adssettings.google.com. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID there also limits the advertising data described above.

Data security and retention

All data leaves your device over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The Service Provider operates no servers and stores no user data. The data described above is held by Google according to its own retention policies. Application settings, such as your best score, theme, and language, are stored only on your device and are removed when the Application is uninstalled.

Deleting your data

Because the Service Provider does not store any of your data, there is nothing held by the developer that can be deleted on request. You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in your Android settings, and you can remove all locally stored data by clearing the Application’s data or uninstalling it.

Children

The Application is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable age of digital consent). The Service Provider does not knowingly collect data from children.
