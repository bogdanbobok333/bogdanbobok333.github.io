Privacy Policy

My Family Tree: Genealogy Book for Windows (Microsoft Store ID 9NJ6NXVR0T60)
Developer: Bohdan Bobok · Effective date: 15 September 2026

This privacy policy explains what data the My Family Tree: Genealogy Book desktop application for Windows (the “Application”) handles, where it is kept, and who it is shared with. Bohdan Bobok (the “Service Provider”) operates the Application as an independent developer.

Data the Application collects

The Application does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. It does not ask you to register, does not create an account, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location. It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

The Application does not use your microphone or camera.

Data stored on your device

The Application is a family tree tool. The information you enter about yourself and your relatives is personal data, and it stays entirely on your computer. It is never uploaded or transmitted anywhere and is not readable by the Service Provider. This data is stored in your Windows user profile, in the Application’s own data folder, and consists of:

  • Family records — names, maiden names, gender, dates and places of birth and death, occupations, biographies, marriages, and the links between parents, children, spouses, and siblings.
  • Photos and document scans — when you add a picture to a person, a copy of that file is saved in the Application’s folder so the record keeps working if the original is moved. The Application only reads files that you explicitly select.
  • Settings — the selected theme, interface language, text size, tree colours, and the positions of cards in the tree.
  • Error log — a local technical log used to diagnose problems. It never leaves your computer.

Files you create on purpose — PDF, TXT and GEDCOM exports and project files (.mft) — are saved only where you choose. What you do with them afterwards, for example sending them to relatives, is up to you.

Network use

The Microsoft Store version of the Application works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

Microsoft Store

The Application is distributed free of charge through the Microsoft Store. Downloads, licensing, and updates are handled entirely by Microsoft. Microsoft Store and Windows may collect data under their own terms:

  • Microsoft Privacy Statement

Data security and retention

The Service Provider operates no servers and stores no user data. Your records, photos, and settings are kept on your computer for as long as you keep them, protected by the security of your Windows user account.

Deleting your data

Because the Service Provider does not store any of your data, there is nothing held by the developer that can be deleted on request. You can delete individual people and photos inside the Application, and you can remove all of its data by uninstalling the Application.

Children

The Application is a general-purpose genealogy tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable age of digital consent). The Application does not collect data from anyone, including children.
