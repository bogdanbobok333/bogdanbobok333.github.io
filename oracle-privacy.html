Privacy Policy

Oracle of the Silver Thread for Android (package com.silverthread.oracle)
Developer: Bohdan Bobok · Effective date: 28 October 2026

Summary

Oracle of the Silver Thread does not collect, store or transmit personal information to the developer. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The deck, the card meanings and the spreads work entirely offline.

The app connects to the internet in exactly two situations, both described below.

What is stored on your phone

Everything the app saves stays in the app's private storage on your own device.

WhatWhy
Settings: language, theme, card back, colours of the description blocks to survive a restart
Spreads you designed yourself, with the names and labels you gave them so you can use them again
Today's card of the day so the card of the day stays the same until midnight
The question you typed before a reading and the cards of the current spread so a reading is not lost when you switch apps
Your Google AI key and the selected model, if you entered them so you do not re-enter the key
"Our apps" list and logos so that screen opens without a connection

None of this is sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in the Android settings, deletes all of it.

When the app goes online

1. AI interpretations

Only if you turn them on in Settings by entering your own Google AI key. When you open a card, the app then sends to Google Gemini: the card's name and the deck's own description of it, the name of the spread and the card's position in it, the interface language, your question if you asked one, and your follow-up questions about that card. Your key is sent to Google only, as proof that the request is yours.

What Google does with the request is governed by its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy and the Gemini API terms.

The developer has no access to these requests and never sees them. Without a key — which is the default — the app makes no such requests at all.

If an AI answer seems inappropriate, the app lets you report it. The report opens your own e-mail app with the answer, the card and the spread filled in; nothing is sent unless you send that e-mail yourself.

2. The "Our apps" screen

When you open this screen, no more than once a day, the app reads the public Google Play pages of apps by the same developer to show their names and logos. The request is anonymous: it contains nothing about you or your device.

What the app does not do

  • does not collect device, user or usage information;
  • shows no advertising and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs;
  • does not share data with third parties;
  • does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts or your files.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.
