Privacy Policy

My Tarot for Windows (Microsoft Store ID 9NX4M09WBZTH)
Developer: Bohdan Bobok · Effective date: 23 September 2026

Summary

My Tarot does not collect, store or transmit personal information to the developer. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The deck, the card meanings and the spreads work entirely offline.

The app connects to the internet in exactly two situations, both described below.

What is stored on your computer

Everything the app saves stays in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MyTarot on your own computer.

WhatWhereWhy
Settings: language, theme, card back, reversed-cards mode prefs\tarot_settings.properties to survive a restart
Your AI provider key and the selected model prefs\tarot_ai.properties so you do not re-enter the key
Cached AI answers prefs\tarot_ai_cache.properties so a repeated reading costs no quota
"Our apps" list and logos our-apps-cache.json, cache\store-icons\ so the screen opens without a connection
Reports you file about an AI answer ai_reports.txt so you can forward them to the developer

None of this is sent anywhere on its own. To delete all of it, delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\MyTarot.

When the app goes online

1. AI readings

Only if you turn them on in Settings and enter your own API key. The app then sends to the provider you chose (Google Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible service whose address you supply): the card name, its position in the spread, the interface language, and your question if you asked one. Your key is sent to that provider only, as proof that the request is yours.

What the provider does with the request is governed by its own policy:

  • Google Gemini — policies.google.com/privacy
  • OpenAI — openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • any other service — the policy of the service whose address you entered

The developer of My Tarot has no access to these requests and never sees them. If AI readings are off — which is the default — the app makes no such requests at all.

2. The "Our apps" list

Once a day the app asks the public Microsoft Store catalogue for the names, prices and logos of apps by the same publisher. The request is anonymous: it contains nothing about you or your computer, only the publisher code and the market code (the country whose price the Store should show). The answer is saved to disk so the screen also opens without a connection.

What the app does not do

  • does not collect device, user or usage information;
  • shows no advertising and contains no advertising SDKs;
  • does not share data with third parties;
  • does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, or files outside its own data folder.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 12 and collects no data from anyone, children included.
